# The Ledger Pricing Strategy

## Overview

The Ledger uses a hybrid pricing model designed around two customer groups:

1. Individual creators who need occasional contract intelligence.
2. Businesses and organizations that manage contracts regularly.

The goal is to keep contract literacy accessible while monetizing high-value professional workflows.

---

# Pricing Model

The Ledger follows a:

- Free access model for creators
- Pay-per-use model for occasional users
- Subscription model for professionals and businesses
- Enterprise model for organizations requiring scale

---

# Creator Plan

## Artist Free

**$0/month**

Designed for independent artists, producers, and creators reviewing occasional agreements.

### Includes

- Limited contract uploads
- AI contract summary
- Risk identification
- Fairness score
- Basic clause explanations
- Contract history

### Purpose

The free creator plan reduces barriers for artists and builds trust before they enter professional agreements.

---

# Creator One-Time Analysis

For artists who only need help with one contract.

## Single Review

**$9.99**

Includes:

- One contract analysis
- Risk report
- AI explanations
- Downloadable summary

---

## Creator Bundle

**$24.99**

Includes:

# Payment Architecture

## Stripe Integration

The Ledger will use Stripe for:

- Subscription management
- One-time payments
- Customer portal
- Invoice generation
- Payment webhooks

Billing events will control:

- User permissions
- Upload limits
- Feature access
- Usage tracking

---

# Feature Access Model

Subscription tier determines:

- Maximum uploads
- AI analysis depth
- Export capabilities
- Team features
- API access

Feature permissions should be controlled through a billing entitlement system.

Example:

User | Subscription Tier | Entitlements | Application Features
