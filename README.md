# The Ledger v2

## AI-Powered Contract Intelligence & Rights Management Platform

> Transforming contracts from static documents into intelligent, actionable business assets.

The Ledger is an AI-powered platform designed to analyze, structure, and extract intelligence from contracts, agreements, and business documents.

Modern businesses depend on contracts for revenue, partnerships, compliance, and operations — yet critical information remains trapped inside unstructured documents.

The Ledger creates a structured intelligence layer that helps organizations understand:

- What rights they have
- What obligations they owe
- Where revenue opportunities exist
- What risks require attention
- How agreements connect across an organization

---

# Vision

Contracts are not just documents.

They are networks of:

- Rights
- Responsibilities
- Payments
- Deadlines
- Dependencies
- Business relationships

The Ledger converts these agreements into structured data that can power better decisions, automation, and operational intelligence.

## 🔗 Rights & Obligations Graph

The Ledger organizes contract information into connected relationships.

Example:

Contract ├── Parties │    ├── Company A │    └── Company B │ ├── Rights │    ├── Revenue Share │    ├── Licensing Rights │    └── Usage Rights │ ├── Obligations │    ├── Payments │    ├── Deliverables │    └── Deadlines

This creates a foundation for future automation and intelligence systems.
